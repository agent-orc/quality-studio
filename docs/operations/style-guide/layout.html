Notices, density, and modal layout

Layout carries hierarchy through alignment, spacing, and restrained tints. Operational content stays compact and actions remain reachable.

Notice bars

An acute notice spans the available content width. Use one status dot, one short fact, and no more than one action. Historical or resolved facts become quiet or disappear.

Do

Do not

Inset card chrome and narrative copy turn one fact into a competing panel.

Spacing and density

Registry modal: internal scroll and sticky footer

Onboard and Configure repository use the standard long-form modal. The dialog is bounded to the viewport. Only the field region scrolls. The footer remains sticky and visible with Cancel and Save in every scroll position.

Behavior contract

PartRequirement
DialogUse a viewport-relative maximum height and a two-row shell below the header.
Field regionUse overflow-y: auto, min-height: 0, and stable scrollbar space.
FooterUse a sticky trailing action row outside the field scroller. Keep Cancel and Save visible.
KeyboardKeep controls in DOM order. Tabbing into a field outside the visible region must scroll that field into view without moving the footer.
Narrow viewportReduce the field viewport, not the action reachability. Preserve the same hierarchy in both themes.

Flat composition

Do not introduce decorative cards around field groups, notices, or modal subsections. Borders remain valid for controls, the dialog boundary, the sticky footer separation, and structures such as fieldsets.

No left accent bars

Status belongs in a tint, badge, or dot. A decorative colored left border is not part of the Quality Studio admin language.

Layout patterns directly apply ADM-01, ADM-02, ADM-08, and ADM-09.
